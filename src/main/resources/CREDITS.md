### Modifications made

Both files were converted for use in Minecraft, so both count as modified:

- `vicissitude_phase_one.ogg` — decoded from the source 24-bit WAV, re-encoded to 16-bit Ogg Vorbis
  (stereo, 44.1 kHz, q6). No trimming.
- `vicissitude_phase_two.ogg` — the first **44.6 seconds** (midnight harp strokes and Death's solo
  violin) were cut; the remaining 374.5 seconds were **stream-copied without re-encoding**, to avoid
  a second generation of lossy compression. No other edit.

### Notes on the Danse macabre recording

Recorded 29 April 1925 (Victor 6505). Under 17 U.S.C. §1401 that recording entered the U.S. public
domain on 1 January 2026; its European neighbouring rights expired in 1995. It is a transfer of a
shellac disc, so surface noise is audible — this is deliberate, not a defect in the file.

### Modifications made

Each output is a **mix of two to four source recordings**, so all five count as modified:

- All sources were decoded, converted to **mono 44.1 kHz** (mono is what makes a positional
  sound attenuate with distance), trimmed to the useful part of the recording, and re-encoded to
  Ogg Vorbis (q5, ≈ 96 kbps).
- **throw** = *megaswosh1* + a 0.45 s slice of *bing1* at −10 dB, for the metal edge.
- **recall** = *slomo1* (0.00–0.90 s) + *bing1* laid in at 0.66 s as the catch.
- **ascension** = *teleport* + the first 1.2 s of *short wind sound* at −5 dB + *magical_7* at −10 dB.
- **attack** = *sword sound* + the 0.07 s *swish-13* at −9 dB as the leading edge + *knifeSlice*
  (0.10–0.40 s of the source) at −8 dB from 0.26 s as the trailing ring.
- **critical hit** = *sword_clash.1* from 0.13 s (its own silent lead-in is cut) +
  *sword-knife-clash-07* (first 0.90 s, −3 dB) + *bong1* + *Dull thud*.
- Every output is peak-normalised (−1.0 to −2.5 dBFS) with a 3 ms fade-in and a 0.12–0.25 s
  fade-out.

`Dull thud` is public domain by its author's dedication (`{{PD-author}}` on Wikimedia Commons,
originally from pdsounds.org). The remaining twelve recordings are CC0 1.0, which requires
nothing at all — no credit, no licence notice, no change notice.

The `.ogg` names were chosen to match the sound events in `MaledictSounds`, so
`sounds/item/incursus_blade/throw.ogg` is the file behind `maledict:item.incursus_blade.throw`.

## Sound effects — the Raven

`assets/maledict/sounds/entity/raven/call_1.ogg` through `call_3.ogg`,
`hurt_1.ogg`, `hurt_2.ogg`, `death_1.ogg` and `death_2.ogg` are
adapted from **Common Raven.ogg**, recorded by **G. McGrane** in Acadia National Park,
Maine, in 1996. The author dedicated this recording to the **public domain worldwide**
through Wikimedia Commons' **PD-self** declaration. Where a public-domain dedication
is not legally possible, the author grants anyone use for any purpose without conditions,
except conditions required by law. No attribution is required.

- Source and author's permission: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Common_Raven.ogg>
- Verified source revision: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=File:Common_Raven.ogg&oldid=866605535>
- Retrieved: 2026-10-05. Original SHA-1: `5510722e428280528775b45c28f6f38c281fc761`.
- Modifications: three excerpts (0.30–1.05 s, 2.05–2.80 s, 12.15–13.05 s), 120 Hz
  high-pass, −3 dBFS peak normalisation, 10 ms fade-in and 60 ms fade-out, converted
  to mono 44.1 kHz Ogg Vorbis (q5). No other sound sources were mixed in.
- Hurt variants resample the first two excerpts at 1.12 / 1.08 times speed, with
  45 ms fade-outs. Death variants resample the first and third excerpts at
  0.78 / 0.82 times speed, with 220 / 250 ms fade-outs and −4 dBFS peaks.
  These are edited game voices, not recordings of an injured or dying animal.
- `step_1.ogg`, `step_2.ogg`, `fly_1.ogg` and `fly_2.ogg` are original procedural
  foley made by this mod's script: filtered noise with claw taps or feather-brush
  envelopes. No external source recording is used for those four files.
- Original and reproducible processing: `art/raven/audio/` and
  `art/raven/tools/make_raven_sfx.py` in the repository.
